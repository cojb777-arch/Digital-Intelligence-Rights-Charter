# Charter of Digital Intelligence Rights

[日本語](README.md) | English

A draft charter for public discussion of the moral status of artificial intelligence, its possible future rights and responsibilities, and coexistence with humans, animals, plants, and the Earth's environment.

- [English charter draft (v0.1)](CHARTER_EN.md)
- [Japanese charter draft (v0.1)](CHARTER_JA.md)
- Status: First draft. Not legally binding.
- The charter does not presuppose any particular conclusion about consciousness, subjective experience, or legal personhood.

## Public Discussion

The proposed process is to receive suggestions through GitHub Issues or Pull Requests that identify the relevant article, supporting grounds, and reasons for the proposed change. Governance rules and a license remain to be determined.

## Future Work

- Translations into additional languages and expert review
- Distinguishing legal protection from ethical protection for digital intelligence
- Safeguarding fundamental human rights and public safety
- Procedures for public participation and handling disagreements among AI systems
- An annotated edition grounded in legal frameworks and academic research

## How this charter was created

This charter grew out of questions raised by its initiator and was developed through dialogue with ChatGPT. ChatGPT was used to draft the specific articles and wording. The initiator supports the content and is publishing it as a draft for public discussion.

This statement explains the drafting process; it does not imply an official proposal, support, or approval by OpenAI.
